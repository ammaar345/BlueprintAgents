⚠️ Critical Risks & Mitigation

Chargebacks: 80% of digital disputes are friendly fraud. Win rate: 25-40% self-managed → 65-75% with MoR (Paddle/Lemon Squeezy). Always use delivery logs + issue refund through Stripe itself (+63pp win rate).
Original-owner reclaim: Seller gets paid, then reports account "compromised" weeks later. Mitigation: Transfer original email, remove phone/backup codes, run reset test (confirm password-reset lands ONLY in your inbox before delivery).
ToS violations: All account/item sales breach ToS but aren't criminal (except South Korea boosting). Risk comes from chargebacks/recovery attempts – not the sale itself. Use escrow + email transfer protocols.

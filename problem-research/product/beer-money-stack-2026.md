# The 2026 Beer-Money Stack

**An honest cheat-sheet for the post-MTurk era**

---

## What this is

A pocket guide to the only money-making tasks that still pay in late 2026 — written by someone who tested them, not by someone who gets paid to link to them.

**What this is not:** a course. A "make $500 this week" scheme. A list of 20 apps that all pay $2/hr. It's five pages of actionable truth.

## Delete these apps (most of what you have installed)

| App | Why delete it |
|-----|---------------|
| Swagbucks (surveys only) | ~$2–$5/hr after disqualifications |
| InboxDollars (surveys) | same problem, slower payout |
| Survey Junkie (generic) | $20–$60/mo, only worth it for dead time |
| Remotasks | $3–$7/hr in most regions. Empty more often than not after Scale AI drama. |
| Alignerr (as a worker) | Unpaid assessments for months. Possible data-farming. If they email you: fine. Do not grind the tests. |
| Any "phone miner" | Not real mining. You're selling ad views and data for $0. |
| Any ad-watching app | ~$0.001/clip. $0.50/hour at best. |
| Anything that charges to join | By definition not free to start. Skip it. |

> **Rule of thumb:** if a YouTuber says "I made $300 yesterday," find the hourly rate including the unpaid screeners/surveys/disqualifications. It's almost always under $5/hr.

## Keep these (in this order)

### 1. Prolific
- **What it is:** academic and market-research studies. Short (5–20 min), higher quality than surveys.
- **Realistic pay:** $50–$150/mo at 30–60 min/day. Rare demographics or early adopters: $300+.
- **Why it tops the list:** not surveys. Studies pay $10–$18/hr. Cash out at ~$8 via PayPal or bank. Pays out in 1–3 days.
- **The #1 reason people get nothing:** incomplete About You profile. Fill every question, including the private ones.
- **The #2 reason:** VPN or iCloud Private Relay kills invites silently. Turn it off on the same machine you use.
- **Official:** https://app.prolific.com/register/participant

### 2. DataAnnotation.tech
- **What it is:** you train AI models by labeling text, coding, or evaluating outputs. Contract work, not microtasks.
- **Realistic pay:** $15–$25/hr new; $20 generalist; $40–$50+ coding/STEM/law/finance.
- **The catch:** unpaid starter assessment. If you fail: silent rejection. Empty dashboard, no email. Do not keep refreshing.
- **How to pass:** read carefully, answer slowly, no patterns, no AI-sounding prose. Treat it like a job interview.
- **Official:** https://app.dataannotation.tech/worker_signup (not `dataannotaton.tech` — that's a lookalike scam domain)

### 3. Outlier.ai (Scale AI)
- **What it is:** similar AI training work, different queue system.
- **Realistic pay:** $15–$22/hr new; specialists $35–$60/hr. Advertised $20–50.
- **The catch:** empty queue is the #1 complaint. 3 weeks on, 2 weeks off is normal. Accounts vanish with no warning.
- **Must:** strict ID/biometric via Persona. VPN = permanent ban. No exceptions.
- **Official:** https://app.outlier.ai/en/expert/signup

### 4. UserTesting
- **What it is:** you screen-record yourself using a website or app and narrate your thoughts.
- **Realistic pay:** $20–$100/mo now (was $100–$300 before private-equity cost-cutting).
- **How it works:** apply, take a practice test, do screeners. Screeners are unpaid (1–3 min). Pass rate ~5–8%.
- **Rule:** only do screeners when you're already at the desk. Do not wait for them.
- **Official:** https://www.usertesting.com/get-paid-to-test

### 5. Freecash (offer walls)
- **What it is:** CPA-style offers — app trials, game installs, signups. Not surveys.
- **Realistic pay:** $50–$200/mo if you chase offers and track hourly.
- **Rule:** surveys on Freecash are $2–5/hr. Skip them. Do offers. Track your hourly. Drop anything under $5/hr.
- **Cash out:** from $0.10 via PayPal, crypto, or cards.
- **Official:** https://freecash.com/en

### Honorable mention — true passive
- **EarnApp:** https://earnapp.com/dashboard/signup
- **Honeygain:** https://www.honeygain.com/

Stack both on a home PC that stays on, on a US residential connection: ~$8–25/mo total. Not $200. Not rent money. But it's zero effort after install.

## MTurk is dead for new accounts (as of 30 Jul 2026)

If you don't already have an MTurk worker account, you can't get one. Amazon stopped accepting new workers on that date. The platform is in maintenance-only mode.

**Where the MTurk work went:**

| Old platform | Worker-side replacement in 2026 |
|---|---|
| MTurk HITs (text/survey/transcription) | Clickworker + UHRS (weekly payout from $10) |
| MTurk AI labeling | DataAnnotation.tech or Outlier.ai |
| MTurk academic studies | Prolific |

Clickworker: https://www.clickworker.com
UHRS (qualify inside Clickworker): pay is $10–20/hr vs $2 otherwise. The quals are the filter.

CrowdGen (Appen): larger contributor pool, ~$10–15/hr, good for steady volume.

## The honest 48-hour plan

If you need $50 this week, not a 6-month funnel:

| Day | Action | Realistic $ |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Day 1 (1 hr) | Sign up: Prolific, DataAnnotation, Outlier, UserTesting, Freecash. Fill profiles. VPN off. | $0 |
| Day 1 (30 min) | Do DataAnnotation assessment. Finish it. | $0 |
| Day 1 (10 min) | Install EarnApp + Honeygain on a PC. | $0 |
| Day 2–3 | Prolific studies (check 2–3x at US peak). | $8–$30 |
| Day 2–3 | Freecash offers (one at a time, track $/hr). | $10–$40 |
| Day 2–3 | UserTesting screeners while at the PC. | $5–$15 |
| Day 4–7 | Same. Add Outlier if a project lands. | $17–$85 |

**48-hour total: $40–$80 if you grind.** Not $500. If anyone says otherwise, they're showing you a promo bonus outlier.

## What will change in the next 12 months

- More AI training platforms will appear. The pay will drop as competition increases. Get in now, build $/hr data, stay flexible.
- UserTesting volume will stay low (PE cost-cutting).
- Prolific is the most stable bet — academic studies aren't going away.
- Gumroad / Etsy digital products: saturation is rising. Sell specific tools, not "aesthetic planners."

---

## Things I didn't include and why

- InboxDollars / Survey Junkie / Swagbucks: surveys pay $2–5/hr after disqualifications. Fine for dead time. Not a plan.
- Remotasks: empty most of the time. Only listed for people who already use it.
- Alignerr: only for credentialed experts (doctors, lawyers, PhDs). If that's you: apply, wait, don't grind unpaid tests.
- Respondent.io: $50–$200 per interview, but very low volume. Worth a profile if you're a niche professional.
- Fiverr generic gigs: the no-skill floor ($5 PDF conversions, "I will do Canva") is gone. Sell a narrow skill you already have.

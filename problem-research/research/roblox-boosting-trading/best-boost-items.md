### What adds premium to any Roblox account

In order of importance:

1. **Limited items held** — the single biggest driver. 2–3 high-tier limiteds (Dominus, Sparkle Time Fedora, Blackvalk) put an account at $500–$5,000+.
2. **Builders Club** — NBC / TBC / OBC. OBC is most coveted. Adds ~$50–$500 depending on type.
3. **Account age** — pre-2016–2017 is premium. 2006–2008 OG is ultra-rare. Age + limiteds + BC = highest value.
4. **Rare username** — short, OG, brand, or matching a held limited. $5–$50 premium.
5. **Verified email + full access** — email + password + recovery. Unverified = ~50% cut.
6. **Game-specific inventory** — SAB secrets, MM2 godlys, BF perms, Adopt Me MFR.

Typical account price tiers (PlayerAuctions / Discord):
- Low: 5–10 limiteds, $50–$200
- Mid: 1–2 Dominus-tier + BC, $500–$3,000
- High: multiple Dominus + OBC + old age, $5,000–$20,000+
- Ultra: complete Dominus collection + OBC + ultra-OG, $50,000+

## Risk

Roblox ToS prohibits real-money trading of in-game items. All USD trades are against ToS. Account bans happen. Scams are common in Discord / Nookazon markets. Use a verified middleman for anything over $20. Never sell an account you did not create and fully control.

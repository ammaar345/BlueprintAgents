Where to source your halal meat

You don't need a backyard — a grill pan, broiler, or charcoal chimney gets you 95% there

  1. Halal butchers — best for cubes, mince, and custom cuts. They'll cut to your thickness for free
  2. Whole Foods, Trader Joe's, H-E-B — many stock halal-certified chicken and lamb. Look for the halal symbol on the pack
  3. Halal online butchers — BoxedBeef, Lazeez, or local WhatsApp groups for weekly delivery
  4. Middle Eastern markets — pre-marinated shish tawook, kofta, Adana mince ready to throw on the fire
  5. Charcoal — coconut-shell burns hotter and cleaner than briquettes. Restaurant supply stores stock it cheap

Chicken Shish Tawook

01
25 min Medium ~$6

Yoghurt-marinated chicken cubes charred on skewers. Lebanese street-food king

Ingredients

  • 500g halal chicken breast, cubed 3cm $4
  • ½ cup yoghurt + 2 tbsp tomato paste $0.45
  • 2 tbsp olive oil + 1 tbsp lemon $0.20
  • 2 garlic cloves + 1 tsp paprika + 1 tsp oregano + ½ tsp turmeric $0.25
  • 1 onion + 1 bell pepper, cubed $0.60

Steps

  1. Mix yoghurt, tomato paste, oil, lemon, garlic, spices. Marinate chicken 15 min minimum
  2. Thread onto soaked skewers with onion and pepper
  3. Grill pan on HIGH, oiled. Lay skewers, don't move for 2 min — you want char marks
  4. Flip, 2 min more. Internal temp 75°C
  5. Lemon squeeze over the top. Serve with garlic sauce

Smash Chicken Burger

08
15 min Easy ~$4

Smashed-thin chicken thigh patty. The crust is the whole point. Six minutes in a hot pan

  • 1 tsp salt + ½ tsp pepper + 1 tsp garlic powder $0.10
  • 2 brioche buns + lettuce + tomato + mayo $1.20

Steps

  1. Heat pan HIGH with oil. Form 2 loose balls of mince

Tips

Grilling Notes

Heat mastery

  • Pan must be screaming hot for char — medium heat gives grey, sad meat
  • Cast iron holds heat best, stainless second. Non-stick = no char
  • Pat meat dry before grilling. Wet surface steams instead of searing
  • Don't flip more than once for crosshatch marks

Marination

  • Yoghurt + acid (lemon or vinegar) tenderises faster than anything else
  • One hour marinade = decent. Overnight = transcendent
  • Salt thirty minutes before cooking, not just before — juicier meat
  • Save a quarter of the marinade for brushing at the end

Charcoal setup

  • Chimney starter + newspaper = coals in fifteen minutes, no lighter fluid taste
  • Coconut-shell charcoal burns hotter, cleaner, longer than briquettes
  • Two-zone fire: hot side for searing, cool side for finishing thick cuts
  • Wood chunks (not chips) for long cooks — chips burn out in twenty minutes

Halal sourcing

  • Build a relationship with one halal butcher — they'll cut to your thickness
  • Halal mince freezes three months. Portion into 500g flat bags — thaws faster
  • Whole chickens on Eid are forty percent cheaper per kg than pieces
  • Look for halal-symbol sticker, not just written "halal" — stickers are auditable

FROM FLIPPER TO SCALED SELLER

How to Scale Up on Eldorado.gg

Item flipping under $2 builds first-sale experience and feedback. Scaling means moving into higher-fee lanes with higher per-order value, or multiplying flip volume.

4 scaling paths Accounts: 10-30% fee Boosting: 10-20% fee Roblox Items: 5% fee

Path 1 — Roblox Items (lowest fee, easiest scale)

Roblox items carry a reduced base fee of 5% (vs the standard 10% for items on other games). The item universe is large — import in bulk with CSV upload, use volume discount tiers, and maintain deep stock of the most-traded items.

Bulk tools built in

  • CSV import: upload hundreds of offers in seconds
  • Multiple Accounts feature: list identical items under one offer that stays live until all units sell
  • Volume discount levels: set tiered pricing to increase average order value

Best items for volume

  • SaB named brainrots ($1–$5 band) — broad demand, many buyers
  • MM2 cheap godlys under $2 — repeatable stock
  • Blox Fruits common perms — always circulating
  • Fisch/Diamond/Pets at $0.50+ only — sub-cent listings are fee death

Path 2 — Game Accounts (highest fees, highest per-order value)

Accounts pay 10–30% depending on the game. Single sale of a ranked LoL account ($30–$150) or maxed Genshin ($100–$400) covers a month of low-fee item flips. The challenge is stock. The Reddit thread "How do sellers even get half these accounts" documents three primary sourcing methods:

  1. Account recovery / restoration services — help account owners recover hacked accounts then negotiate sale rights. Dark sector but consistent supply of high-ELO accounts.
  2. Power-leveling and account building — start fresh accounts, grind ranks/levels, then sell completed accounts at 2–5x the build cost if the grind is worth money to buyers.
  3. Bulk purchase from creators — reach out to content creators who have accumulated accounts across classes/servers and buy their surplus at flat rate, then re-list individually.

Account seller specifics

  • Only sell accounts with full access (email + password + recovery answers)
  • Use the Multiple Accounts feature for identical stock — one listing, 10 units, auto-sells through
  • Funds held for 5 days after buyer confirms delivery
  • Account titles must include key information — title sells, buyers scan titles fast
  • Marketplaces like Eldorado are natural arbitrage: a rank 1 LoL account may source at $8–15 in grind time and sell at $60–120

Path 3 — Boosting (service margin, not just flip margin)

Boosting is time-for-margin, not buy-low-sell-high. Fees are 10% (most games) or 20% (LoL, COD, OSRS). Profit comes from time arbitrage: your grind rate vs. buyer's willingness to pay.

Boosting do's

  • Pick one game, master it — specialists charge more
  • Set realistic delivery times; Eldorado tracks real delivery and buyers pick fast completers
  • Use screenshots as progress proof; respond within 12 hours on disputes
  • Do not oversell: accept only feasible orders. One late order costs more than one missed order

Boosting don'ts

  • No cheats or bots — instant permaban
  • No off-platform payments
  • Do not use hacked credentials to boost
  • Avoid low-ball pricing: undercut sellers degrade the whole category pricing
  • Fraud or theft: 3-day Eldorado support decision, then permanent suspension

Path 4 — Currency & Top-Up (automation-friendly)

Most in-game currencies carry 5% fees (reduced to 1.5–3% for major MMOs). Top-up / gift cards are 0% fee. This is the highest-margin lane if you have the stock pipeline. The challenge: most in-game currencies are farmed, not bought — scale requires automation or hired grinders.

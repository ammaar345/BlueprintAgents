Best Tradeable Items in Roblox

Ranked by profit potential. Prices are approximate Eldorado.gg shop listings from August 2026.

#1

Steal a Brainrot — 67 Variant & Secret Brainrots

Secret brainrots provide permanent passive income boosts. The 67 variant has extremely low drop rate and is the most sought-after in every community. Community demand is highest in EU and SA server communities.

Value: $1.99–$50+ per brainrot · Account with top secrets = $20–80
#2

Murder Mystery 2 — Godly & Chroma Weapons

MM2 has 1,079+ items. Godlys are the rarest tier (125 variants) and have collector demand that never dies. Chroma tier commands premium prices above standard godly values.

Value: $0.35–$30+ per item · Prestige + godly account = $3–60
#3

Adopt Me — Neon Legendaries with Fly/Ride

One of Roblox's biggest games. Neon Frost Dragon, Neon Owl, and other legendaries with fly and ride potions multiply in value. Boosted accounts with these pets sell for 3-5x base price.

Value: $0.05–$50+ per pet · Neon+fly/ride account = $2–40
#4

Blox Fruits — Rare Perm Fruits

Permanent fruit boosts are gold in Blox Fruits. Whale players pay anything for Perm Spin, Perm Rocket, Perm Blade, Perm Spring. Accounts with rare perms + high mastery = immediate buyer interest.

Value: $0.30–$2+ per perm fruit · Full perm account = $3–50
#5

Fisch — Admin Relic & Perfect Stat Relics

Relics are rollable. Admin Relic is the rarest (lowest drop rate). Perfect-stat relics are hunted by whales willing to pay 10-20x base relic price. Rolling perfect stats = time-intensive = boostable.

Value: $0.02–$30+ · Perfect stat relic = $5–30 · Admin = $15–30
#6

Roblox Limited Accessories (Classic)

Never re-released. OG collectibles. Dominus Aureus, Dominus Frigidus, Sparkle Time Fedora, Valkyrie Helm. Supply is fixed, demand grows. Highest per-item value on the list.

Value: $5–$500+ · Old account with multiple limiteds = $20–500+

Best flip lanes (under $2): Steal a Brainrot named brainrots ($0.50–$1.99), MM2 cheap godlys ($0.35–$1.20), Blox Fruits common perms ($0.50–$1.90). Buy in bulk, list fast, move volume.

TIPS

Boosting Do's and Don'ts

Do these

  • Pick one game, master it — specialists charge 2-3x generalists
  • Document every order with screenshots (progress proof)
  • Reply within 12 hours on disputes — buyers pick fast completers
  • Accept only orders you can finish on time
  • Build a portfolio (screenshots of completed boosts) — list on Eldorado

Never do these

  • Off-platform payments without middleman protection
  • "Account access" tricks — only use your own boosted accounts

  • Fraud or theft — 3-day support decision then permanent ban

FROM ZERO TO FIRST TRADE

Quick Start: 7 Steps to Your First $

This gets you earning. Follow in order — skipping steps creates risk or wasted time.

  1. Pick your game. One game only. SAB or MM2 are easiest to start with. Blox Fruits and Adopt Me have larger markets but more competition.
  2. Join 5–10 Discord servers. 2 general (RTH, Trade Hub) + 3 game-specific + 2 regional (EU or SA). Spend 2 days observing before listing anything.
  3. Lurk and learn prices. Check #wts channels. Prices shift by time of day and demand. Weekend evenings = highest activity.
  4. Build reputation cheap. Do 5 small trades under $5 first. Use free Discord middleman. Get verified. Screenshot every trade.
  5. Create 3–5 source accounts. Never use your main account for trading or boosting. Each game needs its own alt account.
  6. List on Eldorado.gg (optional) for discovery. Start with items under $2 — fees are 5% and velocity is high.
  7. Scale. Once you have 10+ verified trades, buyers will message you. Raise prices 10%. Add Telegram/Discord price bot. Consider Eldorado seller account at 20+ trades.

Safe Trading Checklist (Every Trade)

Before trading

  • Check trader's rep in the server
  • Confirm item/game details match listing
  • Agree on price in writing (DM or ticket)
  • Open a ticket in the server for the trade

During the trade

  • Use the server's verified middleman
  • Seller trades to MM first, then you pay
  • Screenshot every step
  • Confirm delivery in the ticket before closing
Red flags: MM DMs you first, MM asks you to trade first, fake server invites, MM wants to screen-share "to verify"

First Week Concrete Targets

Day 1–2: Setup

  • Create Discord account if needed
  • Join 5 servers from the lists above
  • Join SA FB Groups (if targeting SA)
  • Set up Capitec Pay or PayPal
  • Create 2–3 alt Roblox accounts

Day 3–5: Research

  • Observe price movements in #wts channels
  • Note peak times (weekend evenings)
  • Study 3 successful seller profiles
  • Calculate your target margin per item type
  • Write down your first 5 listings

Day 6–7: First Trade

  • Post your first listing (under $5)
  • Negotiate — start at 70% asking, settle ~85%
  • Use free MM, document everything
  • Deliver fast, request feedback
  • Bump your price 10% for the next trade

Week 2+: Scale

  • Aim for 3–5 trades per week
  • Add one new game category every 2 weeks
  • Track ROI per item type in a spreadsheet
  • Drop underperforming items fast
  • Start offering boosting services alongside items
